# GT Medical Technologies

> GT Medical Technologies, Inc. is a Tempe, Arizona medical device company that makes GammaTile,
> an FDA-cleared bioabsorbable collagen implant embedded with Cesium-131 radiation seeds, placed
> into the resection cavity during brain tumor surgery to deliver immediate targeted radiation.
> GT Medical Technologies publishes NO public API, SDK, developer portal, or machine-readable
> specification. This file is generated by API Evangelist from publicly reachable pages; it is
> not published by the company.
